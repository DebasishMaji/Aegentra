# Community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute, ask questions, and disagree respectfully,
regardless of identity, background, or experience. This original project policy
applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and evidence, not a person's worth or identity.
- Be patient with newcomers; make feedback specific and actionable.
- Respect contributor credit, privacy, and existing work in progress.
- Disclose material limitations and distinguish observations from claims.
- Keep discussion relevant; do not flood threads with promotional or automated
  messages.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sharing private
information without consent, personal attacks, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable. Disagreement about a design or finding is not itself misconduct.

## Reporting and moderation

For a nonsensitive moderation concern, ask the maintainer to review the relevant
public thread without repeating harmful or private material. Maintainers may
clarify expectations, request edits, hide/remove disruptive content, or restrict
participation according to severity and repeated behavior. Moderation should
be proportionate and should not punish good-faith technical criticism.

The project does not currently provide a dedicated private conduct-reporting
inbox. For harassment or private-information exposure, use GitHub's **Report
abuse** action or [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Do not put sensitive reports into a public issue, including concerns involving
the maintainer. GitHub moderation is independent of project maintainers.

Security vulnerabilities belong in the [private vulnerability channel](SECURITY.md);
that channel is not a general conduct-reporting service. No response deadline
or absolute confidentiality guarantee is promised by this policy.
